Where this can mislead you

    Method in six lines

    1. Source: the SEC Insider Transactions Data Sets (Form 4), 24 quarters from 2018 Q1 to 2023 Q4. Open-market purchases (code P) of common stock by directors and officers, price at least $1. A joint filing that lists several related owners counts as one insider, and trades over $250 million are dropped as filing typos.
    2. A cluster is three or more different insiders of one company buying within 30 days, at least $50,000 in total. The signal date is the filing date of the buy that completes it. Ninety days of quiet before the next signal at the same company.
    3. Entry is the first close after the filing date, so no trade uses information from before it was public. Exit is 21, 63 or 126 trading days later. Prices are adjusted closes from Yahoo Finance.
    4. Excess return is the stock's return minus SPY over the same dates. The IWM column in the table uses the small-cap index instead.
    5. Single-insider events ($50,000 or more by one person) are a random sample of 3,500, used as a control.
    6. Intervals are 4,000 bootstrap resamples of events. Events overlap in time, so the true uncertainty is wider than shown.
